Who We Are

A business built on purpose and precision

Jansoo Afrik is a Nairobi based distributor of premium sanitary paper and hygiene consumables, serving retailers, institutions and households across Dagoretti, Kiambu and Kajiado. Every product is sourced from trusted factory partners and inspected before dispatch — because quality and consistency are not optional.
