Why Choose Jansoo Afrik?

Flexible Starting Quantities

We support new and growing resellers with practical starting quantities, helping them begin without committing to more stock than their business can handle.

Practical Reseller Guidance

We help informal and emerging resellers understand the sanitary-paper business—from product selection and margins to stock planning and shelf-life awareness.

Household-Friendly Ordering

Households can order quantities that suit their needs. The reseller and institutional bulk-pricing MOQ does not apply to household orders.

Arranged Delivery Support

If you cannot make it to our premises, we help arrange delivery to your location. We coordinate with available local riders, discuss the delivery details with you and help your order reach you without the stress of arranging transport yourself.

Rider availability, delivery charges and timing are confirmed based on the customer’s location and order.

Local Delivery Support

For deliveries within Dagoretti, orders placed by 3:30 p.m. can be arranged for delivery between 10:00 a.m. and 4:30 p.m.

Growing With Our Community

Jansoo Afrik believes hygiene supply should create opportunity as well as meet everyday needs. We support emerging resellers with practical business knowledge and contribute sanitary paper products and hygiene-related support to local NGOs and community initiatives where possible.

Supporting New Resellers

Helping emerging entrepreneurs understand products, margins, stock planning and the practical realities of the sanitary-paper trade.

Sharing Practical Business Knowledge

Providing guidance that many small businesses do not easily access when they are starting and building their customer base.

Supporting Hygiene Initiatives

Contributing sanitary paper products and supporting hygiene-related courses or community programmes through local NGOs where possible.
