Who We Are

A business built on purpose and precision

Jansoo Afrik is a Nairobi based distributor of premium sanitary paper and hygiene consumables. We serve retailers, institutions and households across Dagoretti, Kiambu, Kajiado and the wider metropolitan region — with a commitment to quality, consistency and community.

  • Proudly operating in Nairobi, Kenya.
  • Sourced directly from trusted factory partners.
  • Every product inspected before dispatch.
  • Reliable delivery across multiple zones.
  • Flexible ordering. Start small, grow at your pace.
  • Community and opportunity creation at the core of our mission.
